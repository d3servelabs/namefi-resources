The **.cyou domain** is a short, budget generic top-level domain that [ShortDot SA](https://shortdot.bond/) markets as **"See You"** to a Gen Z and creator audience — but the string itself has a stranger origin: it began life as the NASDAQ ticker of a Chinese online-games company that never used it. This page covers what .cyou is, that surprising history, and the honest reputation trade-offs, verified against IANA, ICANN, and Spamhaus's own reporting.

## What is .cyou?

.cyou is a [generic top-level domain](/en/glossary/tld) with no country tie. ShortDot markets the string as **"See You,"** pitched at creators, influencers, and younger, digital-native brands. As a generic extension, Google treats .cyou like any other gTLD — no automatic ranking boost or penalty and no geo-targeting bias. You can verify the delegation record directly in the [IANA root-zone entry for .cyou](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/cyou.html).

Google also states that [keywords in a TLD do not give any advantage or disadvantage in search](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2015/07/googles-handling-of-new-top-level?hl=en#:~:text=Keywords%20in%20a%20TLD%20do%20not%20give%20any%20advantage%20or%20disadvantage%20in%20search.), so the extension itself receives no inherent ranking boost or penalty.

## History of .cyou

.cyou's history is one of the stranger stories among new gTLDs:

- **Original delegation (2015):** IANA records show .cyou was first delegated on 31 March 2015 to **Beijing Gamease Age Digital Technology Co., Ltd.**, a Chinese online-games operator better known by its brand **Changyou.com** — whose own NASDAQ stock ticker was, fittingly, **CYOU**. The [ICANN Registry Agreement was signed 22 January 2015](https://www.icann.org/en/registry-agreements/details/cyou). In other words, the string didn't originate as "See You" at all — it was the company's own market ticker.
- **Five dormant years:** Changyou never registered a single .cyou domain during its ownership. [Domain Incite's coverage of the deal](https://domainincite.com/25221-possibly-the-strangest-new-gtld-acquisition-yet) noted the divestment coincided with Changyou being reacquired and delisted by its parent, Sohu, making the unused TLD "presumably soon will have no meaning for the company, even defensively."
- **Assignment to ShortDot (2019–2020):** ICANN records show the registry agreement was assigned to ShortDot SA under an agreement dated 9 November 2019, with IANA recording the operational transfer on 14 May 2020.
- **Relaunch as "See You" (2020):** ShortDot ran a sunrise period for trademark holders from 14 May to 15 June 2020, reaching general availability on 23 June 2020, and rebranded the same string as "See You" for a youth and creator audience. Within about four months, [ShortDot reported](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/more-than-30-000-gen-z-brands-and-individuals-from-across-the-globe-have-selected-a-cyou-domain-301159177.html) more than 30,000 registrations and placed .cyou among the "top 10 list of the world's fastest-growing new domain extensions."
- **Where it stands now:** As of this writing, [ntldstats.com](https://ntldstats.com/tld/cyou) reports .cyou at roughly 817,000 registered domains, with about 486,000 — nearly 60% — live in the zone file.

## How people use .cyou

Real, specific niches where .cyou shows up:

- **Creator and influencer pages** — the casual "See You" tone fits personal brands and social-first projects.
- **Youth and Gen Z-oriented brands** — ShortDot's marketing has specifically targeted this demographic since the 2020 relaunch.
- **Budget e-commerce and drop-shipping storefronts** — like other ShortDot extensions, low first-year pricing draws high-volume, price-sensitive registration.
- **Domain hacks** — names that read naturally with the suffix, e.g. `see.cyou`, for the "see you" wordplay.

**Who it's not ideal for:** flagship brands, financial or payment sites, and anything where inbox deliverability is mission-critical. Given the abuse rates documented below, treat .cyou as a secondary or experimental extension rather than a primary business domain.

## Notable sites using .cyou

.cyou does not have widely recognized flagship public sites the way [.io](/en/tld/io) or [.app](/en/tld/app) do. ShortDot's own marketing points to creator and small-brand examples rather than established companies, and Spamhaus's most recent reporting notes a meaningful share of its zone consists of listed malicious or suspicious domains. The honest characterization is that .cyou is used mostly by independent creators, small storefronts, and short-lived projects rather than durable public brands.

## Why choose .cyou?

- **Distinct, casual branding** — the "See You" reading is genuinely on-the-nose for creator and social projects.
- **Open and unrestricted** — no eligibility checks or paperwork required.
- **Budget-friendly** — one of the more affordable extensions, lowering the cost of experimentation.
- **Availability** — short, clean names remain easier to find than in older, saturated TLDs.

## Things to consider

- **Documented abuse history.** Spamhaus's most recent reporting periods have repeatedly listed thousands of .cyou domains as malicious or suspicious — a real trade-off for a legitimate site sharing the namespace.
- **High registration churn.** A large share of .cyou's zone turns over within months, a pattern Spamhaus's own researchers flag as typical of bulk, disposable registration rather than settled use.
- **Lower mainstream familiarity.** Many users still equate "a real website" with .com, costing a sliver of trust at first glance.
- **Unusual etymology.** If you research the string, you'll find it originated as a stock ticker, not the "See You" story — worth knowing before you build a brand narrative around it.

## Who can register a .cyou domain?

**Registration restrictions: open to all.** .cyou is an unrestricted generic TLD — there is no credential, professional-membership, community, or local-presence requirement. Any individual or organization worldwide can register a .cyou name through an accredited [registrar](/en/glossary/registrar).

A trademark sunrise period ran ahead of general availability in 2020, so today registrations are first-come, first-served. The extension supports internationalized domain names (IDNs) and DNSSEC, and standard ICANN lifecycle rules apply — including the auto-renew grace, redemption-grace, and pending-delete periods. The binding rules are set out in the [ICANN Registry Agreement for .cyou](https://www.icann.org/en/registry-agreements/details/cyou).

## .cyou pricing and value

.cyou sits in the budget tier of the gTLD market. Expect **first-year and renewal pricing to differ** — an introductory rate designed to drive volume, against a standing renewal rate that is typically higher, so budget for the renewal price rather than the first-year teaser. The registry may also reserve certain short or dictionary strings as **premium names** carrying higher registration and renewal fees. We quote no figures here; check current rates at the point of purchase.

## Reputation and email deliverability

This is where honesty matters most for .cyou buyers. Spamhaus's [Domain Reputation Update for October 2025–March 2026](https://content.spamhaus.org/4d83c22f-84c5-4f82-95ab-4e6b39f73db5.pdf) reports that .cyou's zone held more than 455,000 domains during the period, of which roughly 4% were listed as malicious or suspicious — and .cyou also ranked among the top TLDs for share of its zone made up of newly registered domains, a churn pattern the report associates with bulk, disposable registration. That said, the same report shows .cyou's absolute count of listed malicious/suspicious domains falling by roughly two-thirds compared with the prior six-month period, suggesting the trend is improving, not static.

Practically, that means some spam filters and cautious users apply extra scrutiny to .cyou traffic even though most individual sites are legitimate. **Mitigation:** configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC properly for any email sent from a .cyou domain, warm up sending volume gradually, keep content clean, and weigh a more established extension for anything trust-sensitive.

## Branding and naming tips

- **Lean into the tone.** "See you" and casual, social-first phrasing are what users will read into the extension — pick a name where that voice fits.
- **Know the real backstory.** If you plan to lean on etymology in your branding, note that "See You" is ShortDot's marketing frame, not the string's original meaning.
- **Avoid look-alike strings.** Confusable characters are exactly what abusive registrants exploit on cheap, high-volume TLDs; a clean, pronounceable name sets a legitimate project apart.
- **Keep it short and speakable.** The main upside of .cyou is availability of concise names — use it for something easy to say aloud on social media.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can anyone register a .cyou domain?

Yes. .cyou is an open, unrestricted generic top-level domain operated by ShortDot SA. There are no credential, membership, or local-presence requirements, so anyone worldwide can register a .cyou name through an accredited registrar.

### Is a .cyou domain safe?

The .cyou extension itself is legitimate and ICANN-accredited, but Spamhaus reporting has repeatedly listed a meaningful share of .cyou domains as malicious or suspicious. The extension does not make a specific site dangerous, but treat unfamiliar .cyou links with the same caution you would apply to any low-cost, high-volume TLD.

### Does a .cyou domain affect SEO?

Google treats .cyou as a generic top-level domain with no automatic ranking boost or penalty and no geographic bias. Its documented abuse history can make some mail and security filters more cautious, which can indirectly affect deliverability and click-through.

### Does .cyou actually stand for "See You"?

Only under its current owner. The string was originally the NASDAQ stock ticker of Changyou.com, a Chinese online-games company that held the domain unused for five years before ShortDot acquired it in 2020 and marketed the same four letters as "See You" for a Gen Z audience.

### Who should register a .cyou domain?

It suits creators, influencers, and youth-oriented brands drawn to the short, casual "See You" reading, plus budget projects where cost matters more than prestige. It is a weaker choice for a flagship brand, financial site, or anything that depends heavily on email deliverability.
