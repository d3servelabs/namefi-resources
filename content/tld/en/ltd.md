The **.ltd domain** borrows the company suffix used across the UK, much of the Commonwealth, and increasingly Chinese exporters: "Limited," or "Ltd." for short. Delegated in the 2012 New gTLD Program, it launched in 2015 under Donuts Inc. and is now operated as Binky Moon, LLC — part of the Identity Digital registry group. Real registrar-level data shows the extension has found particular traction through a China-based registrar, a genuinely distinctive fact for a suffix that reads, at first glance, as a British business term. This page covers where .ltd came from, who operates it, and who it actually suits.

## What is .ltd?

.ltd is a [generic top-level domain](/en/glossary/gtld) delegated under ICANN's 2012 New gTLD Program. As a generic string, it carries no inherent geographic tie in Google's system — per Google's [multi-regional targeting guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites#:~:text=A%20generic%20top-level%20domain%20%28gTLD%29), a gTLD like .ltd needs hreflang, URL structure, or Search Console settings to signal a target country; the suffix itself does not, even though "Ltd." reads as distinctly British in everyday usage.

The registry is Binky Moon, LLC, part of the [Identity Digital](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/ltd.html) group. You can confirm the current sponsoring organization and delegation record in the [IANA root-zone entry for .ltd](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/ltd.html).

## History of .ltd

- **25 September 2014 — ICANN Registry Agreement signed.** Donuts Inc. (through one of its many single-purpose applicant LLCs) executed the original registry agreement, confirmed via the [ICANN Registry Agreement details for .ltd](https://www.icann.org/en/registry-agreements/details/ltd).
- **6 August 2015 — delegated to the root zone**, per the IANA delegation record, followed by the standard sunrise and landrush phases ahead of general availability.
- **March 2018 — contract consolidated under Binky Moon, LLC.** Donuts [scrapped roughly 200 individual applicant shell companies](https://domainincite.com/22675-donuts-scraps-200-companies-consolidates-under-binky-moon) — entities with names like "New Sky LLC" and "Sand Shadow LLC" used only for the ICANN application process — and reassigned their contracts, including .ltd, to a single operating entity, Binky Moon, LLC.
- **June 2022 — Donuts/Binky Moon rebranded as Identity Digital.** The wider registry group, formerly Donuts, now trades as Identity Digital, which continues to operate .ltd under the Binky Moon registry agreement.

## How people use .ltd

- **UK-style private limited companies** — `yourbusiness.ltd` for entities formally registered as a "Limited" company in the UK, Ireland, or Commonwealth jurisdictions.
- **China-based exporters and trading companies** — the extension shows notably strong uptake through China-based registrars (see Notable data below), a pattern consistent with the "limited company" designation used by Chinese export businesses targeting English-speaking markets.
- **International businesses avoiding US-specific suffixes** — founders who find "LLC" or "Inc." read as distinctly American choosing .ltd as a more globally neutral corporate signal.
- **Small businesses wanting a matching-entity address** when their ideal .com is taken.

**Who it's not ideal for:** US-based LLCs or corporations, where [.llc](/en/tld/llc) or [.inc](/en/tld/inc) matches the actual entity type more precisely; and any brand aiming for maximum universal, suffix-agnostic recognition.

## Notable sites and registration patterns for .ltd

.ltd does not have widely recognized flagship consumer brands the way [.io](/en/tld/io) or [.com](/en/tld/com) do, but its registrar-level data reveals a genuinely distinctive fact: according to [nTLDstats' .ltd registry data](https://ntldstats.com/tld/ltd), **Alibaba Cloud Computing Ltd. is the second-largest registrar for .ltd domains by volume**, close behind GoDaddy and well ahead of Western-focused registrars like Namecheap. That is a meaningfully different registrar profile than most gTLDs on this site, and it supports the extension's real-world use by China-connected businesses even without naming individual flagship sites.

## Why choose .ltd?

- **Internationally recognized entity term.** "Limited" is understood across the UK, Ireland, much of the Commonwealth, and by export-focused businesses in China — arguably broader recognition than the US-specific "LLC" or "Inc."
- **Verified registrar-level adoption.** The Alibaba Cloud concentration is a real, checkable data point, not marketing copy.
- **Backed by an established registry group.** Identity Digital (formerly Donuts/Binky Moon) is one of the larger, longer-tenured new-gTLD operators.
- **No formation prerequisite.** As with sibling extensions .llc and .inc, you can register before your company is formally incorporated.

## Things to consider

- **Meaning is entity-specific.** "Ltd." implies a UK-style limited company; a US corporation or LLC using .ltd can read as a structural mismatch to an informed visitor.
- **No formation verification.** Anyone can register regardless of actual "Limited" company status, so the domain alone doesn't prove legal structure.
- **Shell-company contract history.** .ltd's registry agreement passed through Donuts' unusual multi-shell-company application structure before consolidating under Binky Moon in 2018 — not a red flag operationally, but worth knowing if you're evaluating registry stability.
- **Lower mainstream recognition than .com**, particularly outside markets where "Ltd." is a familiar business term.

## Who can register a .ltd domain?

**Registration restrictions: open to all.** [Binky Moon's registry agreement with ICANN](https://www.icann.org/en/registry-agreements/details/ltd) establishes .ltd as a standard, non-sponsored gTLD with no eligibility gate — there is no requirement to be a registered "Limited" company, hold a trademark, or demonstrate local presence. The standard sunrise and landrush phases that ran ahead of the 2015 general availability are long closed, so registration today is first-come, first-served. Standard ICANN lifecycle rules apply, including auto-renew grace, redemption grace, and pending-delete periods, and [WHOIS privacy](/en/glossary/whois-privacy/) is available through most [registrars](/en/glossary/registrar).

## .ltd pricing and value

.ltd follows the typical new-gTLD pricing pattern: **first-year promotional rates commonly differ from the standing renewal rate**, so budget for what the name actually costs to keep long-term. The registry may reserve certain short or high-demand strings as premium names carrying different pricing than standard registrations. No specific figures are quoted here — check current rates with your registrar, and note that pricing may also vary by the registrar's own regional focus given .ltd's China-linked registrar concentration.

## Reputation and email deliverability

.ltd has no documented history of spam or abuse concerns. [nTLDstats data](https://ntldstats.com/tld/ltd) shows the majority of registered .ltd domains active in the zone file, distributed across mainstream registrars including GoDaddy and Alibaba Cloud, consistent with legitimate business use rather than the bulk low-cost registration patterns associated with abuse-prone extensions. Standard email-authentication hygiene (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) remains good practice for any domain regardless of its reputation profile.

## Branding and naming tips

- **Match your actual entity type.** Use .ltd if your business is genuinely a "Limited" company, or if your target market recognizes and expects that designation.
- **Consider your audience's region.** Given its real China-linked registrar adoption, .ltd can be a natural fit for businesses trading with or based in markets where "Limited" and Chinese export branding overlap.
- **Pair with a matching .com where possible**, using .ltd as either your primary regional address or a defensive registration.
- **Avoid it for US-only branding** where "LLC" or "Inc." more accurately reflects your entity type.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can anyone register a .ltd domain?

Yes. .ltd is an open generic top-level domain with no requirement to be a legally registered limited company, hold a trademark, or prove local presence. Any individual or organization worldwide can register one.

### Does a .ltd domain affect SEO?

No. Google treats .ltd as a generic top-level domain with no inherent ranking boost or geographic bias, even though the string reads as a UK-style company suffix. Rankings depend on content, links, and user experience, not the extension.

### Is .ltd popular in any particular region?

Registration data shows notably strong uptake through Alibaba Cloud, a China-based registrar, which is among the top two registrars for .ltd by volume alongside GoDaddy. The "limited company" reading also has natural resonance in the UK, where the term originated.

### Who should register a .ltd domain?

Businesses structured as a UK-style private limited company, or any company wanting the internationally recognized "limited" designation in its address. It suits founders outside the US who find "LLC" or "Inc." less locally familiar than "Ltd."

### Who operates the .ltd registry?

The .ltd registry agreement is held by Binky Moon, LLC, the entity that consolidated Donuts Inc.'s roughly 200 individual gTLD contracts in 2018. Binky Moon is now operated under the Identity Digital brand.
