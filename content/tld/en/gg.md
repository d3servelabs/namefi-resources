The **.gg domain** is the [country-code top-level domain](/en/glossary/cctld/) for Guernsey. Gaming communities also recognise “gg” as “good game.” That reading makes the suffix useful for esports teams, game tools, and community sites.

A gaming association does not remove the registry contract. Before registering a .gg domain, check eligibility, recurring fees, identity requirements, and dispute rules. This guide explains those decisions alongside the branding benefits.

![A game controller and an island connect through a .gg address](../../assets/gg-og.jpg)

## .gg connects a national namespace with gaming

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| TLD type | Country-code TLD for Guernsey |
| Delegated manager | Island Networks Ltd., according to IANA |
| Contracting registry | CI Domain Registry Ltd., according to the registration agreement |
| Registration structure | Direct names such as yourbrand.gg |
| DNSSEC | IANA lists a secure delegation and a root DS record |
| Registration restrictions | Generally open worldwide, subject to identity, reserved-name, sanctions, and use rules |
| Minimum term | One year for registration or renewal under registry terms |
| Best fit | Gaming communities, esports, game tools, and Guernsey organisations |

The [IANA record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/gg.html) establishes the delegation and manager. The [registry agreement](https://channelisles.net/legal/terms-and-conditions-1) establishes the registration conditions. Those sources answer different questions.

## .gg keeps its Guernsey identity

The registry explains that GG and JE originally had reserved code status before ISO fully adopted the codes in 2006. The [operator’s organisation page](https://www.channelisles.net/about-us/more) describes the code history and its Alderney offices.

The suffix belongs to the same broad class as [.io](/en/tld/io/) and [.tv](/en/tld/tv/): country codes that also have meanings for international audiences. However, Google does not treat every such suffix identically.

Google’s [international-site guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites) lists .io and .tv among ccTLDs it treats as generic. The published list omits .gg. That omission supports caution about geographic signals. The omission does not establish that .gg pages cannot rank outside Guernsey, or quantify any ranking difference.

For an international site, plan content language, audience, and language-version URLs before choosing the suffix. A familiar gaming address does not substitute for useful pages.

## Gaming sites demonstrate the suffix’s practical uses

The most useful examples show what visitors actually do on a .gg website:

- **[OP.GG](https://op.gg/)** provides game statistics, rankings, and player lookup tools.
- **[start.gg](https://www.start.gg/)** provides tournament creation, event discovery, and rankings.
- **Discord** documents a `discord.gg/wumpus` invitation example in its [server-joining guide](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034842871-How-do-I-join-a-Server).

Discord’s example confirms an invitation use. An invitation path under `discord.gg` is not a domain registration for the server owner. Registering `yourbrand.gg` gives a separate address that the registrant manages through a registrar.

These examples suggest several practical uses: a tournament organiser can publish schedules, a team can maintain its official site, and a tool developer can publish player statistics. A Guernsey business can also use the national suffix without any gaming theme.

For an audience unfamiliar with gaming, explain the brand clearly. A visitor should understand the service from the page title and content, regardless of the suffix.

## Buyers can compare .gg with nearby alternatives

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical branding association | Gaming and Guernsey | Technology and developer tools | Video and streaming | Broad commercial use |
| IANA classification | ccTLD | ccTLD | ccTLD | gTLD |
| Google explicitly lists it as generic | No | Yes | Yes | Generic TLD |
| Useful decision test | Does the audience recognise gg? | Does the product serve developers? | Is video central to the product? | Does the brand need a broad identity? |

Choose .gg when the gaming meaning helps visitors understand the service. Compare .tv for a video-led channel and .io for a developer product. Check actual name availability rather than assuming that one suffix offers more short names.

## Worldwide eligibility still includes registration conditions

The registry generally permits worldwide registration without a local-presence requirement. However, [agreement sections 17.6–17.9](https://channelisles.net/legal/terms-and-conditions-1) retain identity and sanctions-related restrictions. Reserved names and prohibited uses also apply.

The same agreement sets a one-year minimum registration or renewal period. Registrants must supply accurate information and report changes within seven days. Privacy services must not conceal the registrant’s identity from the registry.

A commercial website must publish identifying business information under section 4.4. Read the complete agreement before launching a store or paid service. General eligibility does not guarantee acceptance of a particular application.

## Renewal and cancellation need an explicit plan

Compare the registration price with the recurring renewal price. Check the purchased term, automatic renewal, cancellation method, and any recovery service in the registrar’s checkout.

The registry agreement describes an ongoing contract with annual fees. Cancellation requires notice before the current annual period ends. The terms allow an additional registrar grace period but do not promise every customer the same recovery window.

Keep account contact details current and record the renewal date separately from the website hosting date. Ask the registrar about its deadlines before transferring a business-critical name. Do not apply a generic .com expiry timeline to .gg without confirmation.

## The registry uses its own dispute process

A free name is not necessarily a name that the applicant has the right to use. Search for conflicting brands before buying a team name, game title, or product name.

The [Channel Isles dispute page](https://www.channelisles.net/legal/dispute-resolution) describes independent arbitration and mediation. Its trademark grounds combine confusing similarity with a lack of rights or legitimate interests, or bad-faith registration or use. Do not replace that test with a summary of ICANN’s UDRP.

The dispute page also permits specified Channel Islands authorities to challenge registrations that could damage the islands’ reputation as a legitimate financial centre. Read the actual procedure when assessing a disputed name.

## A .gg address needs ordinary security and email controls

IANA lists a secure delegation for .gg. That root-level status does not automatically sign every registered name. Ask the registrar whether it supports the DNSSEC configuration required for the individual domain.

The suffix alone does not guarantee email delivery or establish a sender’s reputation. Configure authentication and check the mail provider’s requirements before sending business mail. Avoid promising that a new .gg mailbox will reach every recipient.

For account security, keep recovery details current and restrict access to the registrar account. A Discord administrator’s permissions and a domain registrant’s permissions are separate responsibilities.

## A short name should still identify the project clearly

- Choose a spelling that viewers can type after hearing the address aloud.
- Keep the website name consistent with the team or community name.
- Distinguish a custom .gg website from a Discord invitation path.
- Check conflicting brands before paying for registration or an aftermarket transfer.
- Confirm the renewal cost before printing the address on merchandise.

## Namefi provides a starting point for checking a name

1. Search for the exact `.gg` name at [Namefi](https://namefi.io).
2. Confirm availability, the purchased term, renewal cost, and applicable registry conditions.
3. Complete registration if checkout offers the name, then configure [DNS](/en/glossary/dns/) for the intended service.

Domain registration and website hosting serve different functions. Check the services included in the purchase before launching the site or configuring email.

## Buyers often ask about eligibility and search

### Can anyone register a .gg domain?

Generally yes, without a Guernsey residency requirement. Registry identity checks, reserved names, sanctions-related restrictions, and acceptable-use rules still apply.

### Why do gaming sites use .gg domains?

Gamers use gg to mean good game. That familiar meaning suits game communities, statistics platforms, and esports events, as OP.GG and start.gg demonstrate.

### Does a .gg domain affect SEO?

Google does not include .gg in its published generic-ccTLD list. Treat the Guernsey association as a search-planning consideration, not a prohibition on reaching international readers.

### Who runs the .gg domain?

IANA lists Island Networks Ltd. as the delegated manager. The Channel Isles registration agreement identifies CI Domain Registry Ltd. as the contracting registry.
