This role reports directly to the founder and CEO of Namefi. We evaluate every candidate with the same framework, described in [How We Hire at Namefi](/en/careers/how-we-hire). This post lists only what is specific to this role. Unlike most roles at Namefi, this role has fixed working hours.

**We fill this role only through EA agencies.** The EA works as a contractor through the agency. We do not contract individual EAs directly for this role. If you are an individual EA, please apply through your agency.

## What You Will Do

- Make phone calls for the CEO to banks, insurers, medical providers, government agencies, airlines, hotels, and partners. Set up three-way calls when the account holder must confirm.
- Manage the calendar, meetings, and travel of the CEO.
- Handle finance and administration: check the bookkeeping categories that AI agents fill in and set the rules, prepare payments in Mercury for approval, and track receipts and invoices in QuickBooks.
- Support growth and social media work, and coordinate personal and family matters for the CEO.
- Use AI tools every day to draft, research, and report. Check AI output before you use it, and add what AI agents cannot: judgment and quality control.

## Working Hours and Availability

- **Hours:** Weekdays, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time: eight working hours plus a one-hour meal and nap break.
- **Flexibility:** At times, at the request of the CEO, shift the whole window up to 3 hours earlier or up to 3 hours later, for example 5:00 AM to 2:00 PM or 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM Pacific Time.
- **Standby:** During working hours, stay in a Slack huddle so that the CEO can talk to you at any time. Answer Slack calls immediately, Slack messages within 2 minutes, and emails within 30 minutes.
- **Breaks:** Say so in Slack before a meal or a break, and again when you return.
- **Travel:** The CEO travels to the US East Coast, Asia, and sometimes Europe. During travel, move the working hours to the local time of the CEO, with the same eight hours plus a one-hour meal and nap break.
- **Leave:** Give at least two weeks of notice for planned leave. For sudden sick leave, tell the CEO before the workday starts, and hand over the items due that day. The agency approves your leave.
- **Backup:** For absences of more than three days, the agency provides a backup EA.

## What We Look For

- **Experience:** At least 2 years or 4,000 hours of executive assistant experience.
- **Reliability:** Be online and responsive during the agreed hours. Own each task: push it forward, or tell the CEO honestly where it is stuck.
- **Follow-through:** Give an estimated completion time for each task and follow up yourself. The CEO should never need to ask for status.
- **Persistence:** When the other party does not respond, change channels, ask for a supervisor, and follow up until there is a result.
- **Phone skills:** Fluent spoken English. Handle calls with US institutions on your own.
- **Judgment in writing:** Fit the message to the audience: long-term vision for investors, immediate value for customers, established industry practice for compliance partners. Keep every version honest.
- **Working under pressure:** Stay calm, set priorities, and communicate clearly in stressful situations.
- **Attention management:** Help the CEO focus on the decisions that need attention. Handle the rest on your own and follow through.
- **Attention to detail:** Check details, know basic financial processes, and use checklists to do things right the first time.
- **Security and confidentiality:** Use a password manager for credentials. Never send passwords in plain text. Keep company and personal information confidential.
- **Reusable knowledge:** When a task will repeat about 10 times, write an SOP for it. Ask your AI agent to summarize what you did and what to improve, and save the result as a skill for the team.

## Nice to Have

- Experience supporting a startup CEO.
- Experience with Mercury, QuickBooks, Gusto, Stripe, ClickUp, and Google Workspace.
- Experience with US health insurance, taxes, corporate compliance, or insurance claims.
- Knowledge of the crypto, Web3, or domain industry.
