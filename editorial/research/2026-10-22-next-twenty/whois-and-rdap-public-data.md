# Public-data research preserves the G29 privacy scope

Sources opened October 21, 2026: current ICANN Registration Data Policy sections 9.1 and 9.2 (page carries May 12, 2026 revision), ICANN RDAP user guidance, RFC 9082 section 3.1.3, RFC 9537 section 3 and redaction methods, ICANN RDRS participation guidance. The manuscript distinguishes gTLD obligations from country-code policies, held data from published data, protocol from presentation, and redaction from failed lookups. It makes no universal WHOIS shutdown claim or disclosure entitlement claim.
