Editorial Pipeline编辑流水线

How a topic becomes a verified, localized, distributed page, and who decides what. Dotted terms open notes.一个选题如何变成经核实、已本地化、可分发的页面，以及每一步由谁拍板。带点线的词悬停可看注解。

Principles原则

  • 1We compile and verify; we do not originate.我们整理编译并核实，不做原创报道。 Source sufficiency is checked before a topic is green-lit.信源是否足够，在立项前就判定。
  • 2Accuracy before speed.准确先于速度。
  • 3Agents draft, machines validate, humans decide.agent 写，机器验，人拍板。 Five human gates, everything else automated.五个人工闸口，其余全自动。
  • 4The English fact list is the source; every locale is a native rewrite.英文事实清单是源；每个语言都是母语式整体重写。
  • 5Every claim cites, every page timestamps, no number is invented.每条论断有引用，每页有时间戳，数字绝不编造。
  • 6Corrections are public, labeled and propagated.更正公开、标注、同步到所有语言。
  • 7Editor attention is the scarce resource.编辑的注意力才是稀缺资源。 The scheduler budgets it alongside model capacity.调度器把它和模型产能一起记账。

Backlog ranking · v0选题排序 · v0

Rank by expected clicks. Nothing else, for now.现阶段只按预期点击排序，不看别的。

expected_clicks(topic) = Σlocale Σkeyword volume × CTR(expected position) expected_impressions(topic) = Σlocale Σkeyword volume × [position ≤ 10] rank key = expected_clicks ↓ tie-break = expected_impressions effort → capacity fitting only (never in the rank key)
  • aOnly impressions and clicks count at this stage.现阶段只算曝光与点击。 Fit, urgency and strategy weights are parked as v1.契合度、紧迫性、战略权重留作 v1。
  • bCTR curve starts from a published study, then switches to our own Search Console curve after 12 weeks.CTR 曲线先用公开研究，12 周后换成我们自己 Search Console 的实测曲线。
  • cExpected position is the weak link: existing pages use real data, new topics are calibrated per cluster.预期排名位是最弱一环：已发布页用真实数据，新选题按 cluster 校准。
  • dTwo weekly budgets: agent work units and editor minutes.两个周预算：agent 工作单位与编辑阅读分钟数。 Fill in rank order; skip what does not fit.按名次装箱，装不下就跳过。
  • eTopics without measurable demand (most timely pieces) enter only by editor override at G1, logged.量化不了的选题（多数新闻快评）只能在 G1 由编辑插队，并记录原因。
  • fProgrammatic families (TLD pages) are scored as a batch.模板化页面族（TLD 页）按批次打分。 Refresh-due pages compete in the same list.刷新到期页在同一榜单竞争。
